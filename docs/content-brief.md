## 4. Main Language Rule
- Arabic RTL is the main explanatory language.
- English is used for labels, subtitles, names, categories, strategic terms, company names, and executive metadata.
- Do not make the project Arabic-only unless requested.
- Do not make the project English-only unless requested.

## 9. Things to Avoid
- Do not make the page look like SaaS.
- Do not use icons/emojis unless asked.
- Do not write cheap promotional copy.
- Do not use “Subscribe Now” for memberships.
- Do not show top-tier membership as a normal product.
- Do not overuse “VIP”.
- Do not make unverified factual claims.
- Do not invent sources.
