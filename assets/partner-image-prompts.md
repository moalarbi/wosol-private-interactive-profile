# WOSOL Partner Ecosystem Image Prompts

Use these prompts to generate new images specifically for the `Partners & Access Ecosystem` cells.

## Shared Generation Rules

Aspect and layout:
- Target composition: landscape `8:5`, suitable for ecosystem cells.
- Recommended output size if the image tool supports it: `1600 x 1000 px` or `1920 x 1200 px`.
- Keep the main subject centered with generous safe margins on all sides.
- Avoid tight close-ups so the image can sit inside a cell without important details being cropped.
- The final CSS should use `background-size: contain` or an image layer with `object-fit: contain` to prevent cropping.

Visual style:
- Same style family as the current service images.
- Realistic editorial luxury photography.
- Cream-first, deep navy, muted champagne gold.
- Quiet, premium, private, boardroom-ready.
- No visible brand logos.
- No readable text.
- No watermark.
- No crowded scenes.
- No people posing toward camera.

Strict alcohol rule:
- No wine, champagne, cocktails, bar counters, bottles, glasses that imply alcohol, or beverage service associated with alcohol.
- For dining or hosting scenes, use water glassware, porcelain, linen, dates, Arabic coffee pot silhouette without visible branding, flowers, tableware, or menu cards with no readable text.
